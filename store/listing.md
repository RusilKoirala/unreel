# Chrome Web Store listing

## Name
Unreel: Hide Shorts, Reels & Feeds

## Summary (132 characters max)
Hide YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and endless feeds, all day or during hours you set. Free and open source.

## Category
Productivity > Tools

## Description
You opened YouTube for one video. Forty minutes later you're still swiping.

Unreel removes the parts built to keep you scrolling and leaves the parts you came for.

On YouTube, hide:
- Shorts, everywhere they appear: home, search, sidebar and channel tabs
- The home feed
- Suggested videos beside the player and cards at the end of a video
- Comments (optional)

On Instagram, hide:
- Reels
- Explore
- The home feed and stories

What keeps working: YouTube search, subscriptions and playlists. Instagram messages and profiles. A Short someone sends you opens in the normal player, without the endless swipe. A Reel sent to you directly still opens.

Pause when you really need to. A pause lasts five minutes and starts after a ten-second wait, which is often long enough to change your mind.

Block hours: block during class or work and leave your evenings alone. Set different hours for different days, including overnight.

Free and open source: no account, no analytics, no tracking, no paid tier. Unreel runs in your browser and never records what you watch. Read the code at https://github.com/RusilKoirala/helius

Unreel is an independent tool and isn't affiliated with YouTube or Instagram.

## Single purpose (dashboard field)
Unreel hides distracting content on YouTube and Instagram, such as Shorts, Reels and feeds, always or during hours the user sets.

## Permission justifications (dashboard fields)
- storage: Saves the user's rule choices, block hours, pause state.
- Content script on youtube.com and instagram.com: Hides the content the user chose to block on these two sites. Page data is read only on the device.

## Remote code
No. All code ships in the package.

## Data disclosure (dashboard)
- Collected: nothing. Settings stay in Chrome storage.
