# Unreel privacy policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Unreel hides parts of YouTube and Instagram in your browser. It has no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking, and nothing it handles leaves your browser. The source code is public, so you can check this yourself: REPO_URL

## What Unreel stores

Your rule choices, block hours and pause state are saved with Chrome's storage. If you have Chrome sync turned on, Chrome syncs them between your browsers.

## What Unreel reads

Unreel checks the address and page structure of youtube.com and instagram.com tabs to decide what to hide. This happens only on your device. Unreel does not record or send your browsing activity, and it does not read your messages, posts or account details.

## Removing your data

Uninstalling Unreel removes the data it stored in your browser.
